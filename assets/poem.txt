They say to me, a rose with a long stem is a rose begging to die.

"Water can't reach the petals" They say
"Any wind can snap the flower" They say

I was apt to agree. But yet it stood there. Shadowing everything I have ever known. Its petals never drop. The resplendent red shadows it casts when shone by the sun. I thought it was just a small rose with an overgrown stem. It turned out to be everything that I have ever known. 

"How can anyone say this beautiful rose is dying?" I say. Unknowingly my hand was already holding the rose. I tried to put it down, yet it was moving with my hand. I then see the serpentine stem was entwining my arms. Somehow I never realized, the stem was enveloping my whole body. Its thorns seeping deep inside my skin. Yet it didn't hurt.

My wonder wanders, I seek to know. What could this resplendent rose be feeding upon? I search for its root. Only to realize the base of the stem grafted to my chest. I finally see. The rose can never die. Not as long as my heart beats. It didn't matter how long the stem is. I am always keeping it alive. My blood can always reach its petals.
